20Focus Support

20Focus reminds you to follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Restore a subscription

On a new or reset iPhone, open 20Focus and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or in Settings. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose 20Focus. Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before your trial or billing period ends to avoid being charged.

Reminders aren't showing up

Check that notifications are on for 20Focus in Settings > Notifications, with Time Sensitive Notifications allowed. If you use a Focus mode, allow Time Sensitive notifications in that Focus too.
